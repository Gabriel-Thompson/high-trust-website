Flagship work

The American High Trust Study.

A defensible account of the rise and decline of high trust in the United States.

The study is intended to establish a timeline, test competing explanations, identify turning points, and separate causes from symptoms. State Trust Profiles will then show how those forces differ across the country.

Research standard

Simple does not mean sloppy.

Sources

Show the evidence.

Important factual claims should point to sources. Measured facts should be separated from judgment and recommendation.

Consistency

Use the same rules.

Compare states and time periods consistently. Publish assumptions behind rankings, measurements, and scores.

Integrity

Correct the record.

Correct errors publicly and do not change a standard because it produces an inconvenient political result.
