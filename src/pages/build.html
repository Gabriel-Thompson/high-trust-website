Pillar two

Build.

Turn the analysis into a governing plan that can actually be used.

The governing product

The High Trust Roadmap.

Research only matters politically if somebody can act on it.

The Roadmap will bridge research and advocacy. It should be specific enough to govern from, flexible enough to adapt to different states, and short enough that a serious candidate can understand the whole thing.

The goal is not to hand a candidate a philosophy book. The goal is to hand a leader a roadmap.

Every major recommendation

Six questions before we call it policy.

  1. What is the problem?
  2. What evidence tells us it matters?
  3. What action should government or civil society take?
  4. Who has the authority to act?
  5. What result should we expect?
  6. How will we know whether it worked?
Possible structure

The territory of a High Trust agenda.

The research comes first. These categories describe the territory—not a fixed platform written before the evidence.

Public order

Safe communities

Restore predictable public order and focus consequences on serious and repeat offenders.

Family

Strong families

Remove unnecessary pressures against family formation and support institutions that help parents raise capable children.

Integrity

Honest systems

Detect and punish fraud, corruption, abuse, and organized exploitation of public and private systems.

Competence

Government that works

Define core duties, measure performance, simplify bureaucracy, and attach consequences to repeated failure.

Education

Education for adulthood

Prepare students for work, citizenship, family life, and responsibility—not simply credential accumulation.

Opportunity

Work & ownership

Protect the ability to work, build, save, start businesses, own property, and move upward.

Make it usable

From finding to implementation.

  • Short policy briefs for individual issues.
  • Model legislation where legislation is the answer.
  • Executive-action options for governors and agency leaders.
  • Local-government options for mayors, sheriffs, school boards, and county officials.
  • A full governing platform for candidates who want to run on a High Trust agenda.
  • Implementation checklists so a good idea does not die after the press conference.
