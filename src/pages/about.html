About us

A conservative project for American self-government.

The purpose is not to demand confidence. It is to make people, systems, and institutions more worthy of it.

What we believe

Freedom requires a culture capable of sustaining it.

America was founded on the belief that free people can govern themselves.

Constitutional government depends on citizens who can keep commitments, raise families, work, serve their communities, respect the rights of others, and accept lawful consequences when they violate the rules. It also depends on institutions that apply the law fairly, spend public money carefully, tell the truth, and perform the jobs they were created to perform.

We will say plainly

Our principles.

  • Freedom requires responsibility.
  • Rights and duties belong together.
  • Stable families and strong communities are public strengths, not merely private preferences.
  • Equal justice requires rules that are enforced rather than selectively ignored.
  • Markets depend on honest dealing and meaningful consequences for fraud.
  • Government should do fewer things poorly and the necessary things well.
  • Local knowledge and voluntary institutions should be protected whenever they can solve a problem better than distant bureaucracy.
  • Trust should follow trustworthy behavior. It should never be demanded as an act of political loyalty.
Guardrails

What High Trust America is—and is not.

We are

A conservative organization.

We make a clear case for ordered liberty, limited and competent government, strong families, public order, honest markets, personal responsibility, local institutions, and constitutional self-government.

We are not

A party committee.

The mission is larger than any party, candidate, or election cycle. The organization should never become an appendage of a politician.

We are not

An academic club.

Research quality matters, but the purpose is to understand reality and produce action—not impress people with difficult writing.

We are not

A “trust us” campaign.

When distrust is rational, the answer is reform—not better messaging.

We are not

A grievance machine.

We will identify failure plainly, but outrage without a usable solution is not enough.

We are

Willing to learn.

If evidence shows one of our recommendations does not work, we should change it.

The organization

A group of people capable of doing the work.

High Trust America should begin as a small core team surrounded by a larger network of serious contributors.

Researchers. Policy builders. Operators. Communicators. Advocates. Members and volunteers. Serious without being self-important. Conservative without being scripted. Focused on building things leaders can use.
